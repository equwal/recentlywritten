README (1153 bytes)

1 Keyboard layouts for Minimak 4, 8 and 12 on Linux
2 
3 See minimak.org for details.
4 
5 TTYs (with .map files) and graphical X keymaps (with .xkb) are supported.
6 
7 ```sh
8 # In a TTY
9 $ sudo loadkeys minimak12.map
10 # In an X session
11 $ xkbcomp minimak12.xkb $DISPLAY
12 ```
13 Also a good idea to put these commands in /etc/profile and ~/.xinitrc
14 for setting at startup.
15 
16 Some extra layouts set Caps Lock to ESC, swap [] with (), rctrl as a "compose key", and flip punctuation and numbers.
17 
18 
19 These are the supported layouts as of v1.0, feel free to submit truex ate equwal doot com.
20 
21 XKB layouts:                | 4 | 8 | 12|
22 default                     | v | v | v |
