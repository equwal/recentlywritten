1 # Rebind: Button & Key Remap
2 
3 Rebind remaps the hardware buttons of an Android 12+ device: volume keys, the
4 Power button, page-turn buttons, camera and assistant keys, and the AI key of
5 the Viwoods AiPaper. It is made for e-ink readers first, and it works on any
6 Android phone or tablet.
7 
8 **Free beta.** Download the APK from
9 [Releases](https://github.com/equwal/rebind/releases). Everything works at no
10 charge while the beta is open. On a device with no Google Play, the app stays
11 free and complete after the beta too.

24 ## What it does
25 
26 - Tap the button in a drawing of your device. Choose tap, double tap, triple
27   tap or hold. Choose what it does. Rebind asks only for what that needs.
28 - Two buttons together make a combination.
29 - Actions: Back, Home, Recent apps, page turn (swipe or scroll), voice typing,
30   lock screen, screenshot, open an app or a shortcut, media keys, brightness,
31   a menu of actions, and more.
32 - The Power button: hold and double press work on any device. With
33   [Shizuku](https://github.com/RikkaApps/Shizuku), every Power press works:
34   tap, double tap, triple tap, hold.
35 - An on-screen button that floats over every app, for devices with few buttons.
36 - Recent apps made for e-ink: a button opens
37   [Ink Recents](https://github.com/equwal/ink-recents), our free and open
38   source recent-apps app. Cards with no glide, swipe up to close an app, swipe
39   down to close all the others.
40 - Carries apps of other makers, not changed, and installs them when you ask:
41   inkOS and ThinkLauncher (home screens made for e-ink, GPL-3.0), Ink Recents
42   (GPL-3.0) and Whisper (speech to text on the device, MIT). Android asks you before each
43   install. Rebind downloads nothing: it has no internet permission.
44 - Navigation in any mix of button bar, gestures and the Power button (needs
45   Shizuku or an adb grant).
46 - Extra-dim frontlight below the lowest system level (needs Shizuku with root).
47 - Export and import of all settings as one JSON file. Rebind also keeps an
48   automatic copy in `Documents/Rebind/`, which stays after an uninstall.
49 - Updates: [Ink Update](https://github.com/equwal/ink-update) tells you when a
50   new version is ready. It is an app of its own, so that Rebind needs no
51   internet permission.
52 
53 No ads. No internet permission. Nothing is collected. See
54 [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

61 ## Install
62 
63 1. Download `rebind-<version>-full.apk` from Releases and open it.
64 2. Open Rebind. Tap a button in the drawing and follow the steps.
65 3. If the accessibility switch turns itself off: App info > three-dot menu >
66    Allow restricted settings. Android does this to every app that is not from
67    a store.
68 
69 Before you uninstall: if Rebind handles the Power button, turn that off first
70 (Advanced > Power button), so that the system settings go back as they were.
