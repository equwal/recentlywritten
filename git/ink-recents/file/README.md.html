1 # Ink Recents
2 
3 A recent-apps switcher drawn for e-ink.
4 
5 The screen shows one app card at a time. Swipe sideways to go to the next card.
6 Tap a card to open that app. Swipe a card up to close that app. Swipe a card
7 down to close all the other apps. Two tall buttons do the same thing with one
8 tap.
9 
10 Nothing animates. The row jumps from card to card in one step, because e-ink
11 shows a moving row as a smear.
12 
13 Made for e-ink readers (Viwoods, Onyx Boox, Bigme and others) and any
14 Android 12 or later.

25 ## Install
26 
27 Download the APK from the Releases page and install it.
28 
29 The app needs one of the two modes below. Open the app and follow the screen.
30 
31 ## The two modes
32 
33 Android does not tell an app what is running. So the app works in one of two
34 ways.
35 
36 ### Usage access
37 
38 You give the permission in Android settings. The app reads the usage log and
39 puts your apps in order of last use. A card shows the app icon. "Close" ends
40 the background processes of the app, and the app leaves the list until you open
41 it again.
42 
43 ### Shizuku
44 
45 Shizuku is a separate free app. You start it from the device through wireless
46 debugging. There is no computer and no root.
47 
48 With Shizuku the app reads the real task list of the system. A card closes its
49 task. Where the shell is root, a card also shows the picture that the system
50 keeps of the task.
51 
52 The settings screen has the four steps and a button for each one.
53 
54 ## Open the screen from somewhere else
55 
56 Bind a key to this intent action with a key remapper:
57 
58     dev.equwal.inkrecents.OPEN
59 
60 Example with adb:
61 
62     adb shell am start -a dev.equwal.inkrecents.OPEN
63 
64 There is also a quick settings tile. Its name is "Recent apps". Add it to the
65 quick settings panel, then tap it.
66 
67 ## Open it with a hardware button
68 
69 [Rebind](https://github.com/equwal/rebind) remaps the buttons of an e-ink
70 reader or any Android device. It can put this screen on a button: for example,
71 a double tap of Power. Rebind is from the same maker.
72 
73 More extensions: [Awesome Rebind](https://github.com/equwal/awesome-rebind).

80 ## Build
81 
82 You need JDK 17 or later and the Android SDK, with platform 36.
83 
84     ./gradlew testReleaseUnitTest assembleRelease
85 
86 The APK is in `app/build/outputs/apk/release/`.
87 
88 Release signing is optional. Put a `keystore.properties` file in the root of
89 the project with `storeFile`, `storePassword`, `keyAlias` and `keyPassword`.
90 Without that file the build makes an unsigned APK.
