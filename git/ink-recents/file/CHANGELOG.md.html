7 ## 0.1.0
8 
9 First release.
10 
11 - Recent apps as cards, one card for each swipe, drawn for e-ink.
12 - Tap a card to open the app.
13 - Swipe a card up to close that app.
14 - Swipe a card down to close all the other apps.
15 - Two buttons: "Close all but <app>" and "Close all".
16 - A strip of icons shows every recent app. A tap on an icon brings its card to
17   the middle.
18 - Two modes: usage access, or Shizuku for the real task list.
19 - A quick settings tile, and the intent action `dev.equwal.inkrecents.OPEN`.
20 - A settings screen with the state of each mode and the setup steps.
