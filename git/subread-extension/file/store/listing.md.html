20 ## Description
21 
22 ```
23 Read along with an audiobook on YouTube, with subtitles whose words come from the book itself.
24 
25 1. Open the video. Press SR at the top right of the player.
26 2. Choose the .srt of that recording.
27 3. If the video has an intro that your file does not have, play to the first line and press "First line is now". A speed control is there for an upload that runs a little fast or slow.
28 
29 Turn on "Transcript" to see all the lines next to the video. Click a line to jump to it.
30 
31 The subtitles and the settings are kept for each video, so they are there the next time.
32 
33 No subtitles yet? https://subread.space makes them from the audiobook and its ebook: free, in your browser, nothing uploaded. A small speech model only listens for where the narrator is; the text of each line is the book's own, so names, spelling and punctuation are right. For language learners, and for anyone who wants text and voice together.
34 
35 Private: the extension does not read, record or download the video or its sound, and it sends nothing anywhere. Its one permission keeps your subtitles in your browser. Open source (AGPL-3.0).
36 ```

48 ## Graphics

50 Upload the four `store/graphics/screenshot-*.png` (1280 x 800) to both stores.
51 They show two YouTube audiobooks with the subtitles in `store/demo/`:

53 - Moby-Dick, LibriVox, read by Stewart Wills:
54   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PsNsjxIOcmw (text: Project Gutenberg #2701)

56   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPXpHYs7y3s (text: Aozora Bunko 773)

58 The times of the lines come from the YouTube transcript. The words come from
59 the book.
60 
61 To take them again, start Brave with the extension and a CDP port, then run
62 `tools/shots/shot.mjs` for each shot:
63 
64 ```
65 brave --user-data-dir=%TEMP%\subread-shots --remote-debugging-port=9334 --load-extension=<this folder>
66 node tools/shots/cdp.mjs tools/shots/shot.mjs <video url> <absolute .srt path> <seconds> <out.png> <panel 0|1>
67 ```
68 
69 `python tools/graphics.py` makes `promo-440x280.png` (small promo tile) and
70 `marquee-1400x560.png` (marquee promo tile) for Chrome. The icon is
71 `icons/128.png`.
