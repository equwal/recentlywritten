14 <h1>SubRead: your subtitles on YouTube</h1>
15 <ol>
16   <li>Open a video on YouTube, for example an audiobook.</li>
17   <li>Press <b>SR</b> at the top right of the player.</li>
18   <li>Choose the <b>.srt</b> of that recording. If the video has an intro, play to the first line and press <b>First line is now</b>.</li>
19   <li>Check <b>Transcript</b> to also show your subtitles as a transcript panel next to the video.</li>
20 </ol>
21 <p>No subtitles yet? <a href="https://subread.space" target="_blank" rel="noopener">subread.space</a> makes them from
22   the audiobook and its ebook, free, in your browser. The words come from the book, so names and spelling are right.</p>
23 <p class="muted">The extension does not read, record or download the video. Your subtitles stay in this browser.
