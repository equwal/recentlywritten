Add a SubRead transcript panel, filled with the loaded SRT

Add a Transcript checkbox next to Show. When on, it builds SubRead's
own transcript panel (not YouTube's), lists every cue with its time on
the video's clock, highlights and scrolls to the cue now playing, and
seeks the video when a line is clicked.

Place the panel at the top of the related-videos column. When that
column is not there, place it below the player instead. Keep the
panel through YouTube's page changes, and take it down when the
subtitles are removed.
