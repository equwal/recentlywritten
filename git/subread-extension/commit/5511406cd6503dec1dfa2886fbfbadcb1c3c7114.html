Test the subtitle reader with the text of five languages

The reader must give the text of a cue back as it is. Only English and
Japanese had a test, so a defect in another language was not found by a test.

Add a case that reads a file with lines of English, Portuguese, Spanish,
Russian and Japanese, and asks for the cue of a moment in each line. The
accents, the inverted marks, the guillemets and the ideographic space must
come back unchanged.

+  assert.deepEqual(cues.map((c) => c.text), Object.values(lines));
