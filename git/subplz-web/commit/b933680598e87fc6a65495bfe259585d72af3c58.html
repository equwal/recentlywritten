Sell ten books for $4.99

The owner changed the price: $4.99 now buys ten books on the server, not
one. The pack of ten is the only plan for sale.

The earlier plans (one book $4.99, five $16.99, twenty $39) are not sold
now. They stay in RETIRED_PLANS, and fulfilment still finds them: a buyer
who opened the payment page before the change gets what that page showed.
