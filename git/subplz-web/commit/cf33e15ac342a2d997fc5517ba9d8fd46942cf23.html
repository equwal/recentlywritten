Give free credits to verified emails: 2, then 1 a day, and 1 per 2 books

The owner set new free credits for the server:

- A visitor without a verified email gets one free credit, as before. A
  buyer whose email came only from Stripe is in this group, so paying no
  longer unlocks the account credits.
- An email is verified when its owner opens a sign-in link that the
  server sent to it (Account.email_verified). A verified account gets two
  free credits in all, one more each day (not carried over), and one more
  for every two books that the server converted for it. Books converted in
  the browser do not count, because the server cannot check them.
- A server job spends the daily credit first, then a free credit, then a
  bought one. A failed job gives the daily credit back on the same day.

The page offers "Sign in for 2 free credits + 1 a day", and "Verify your
email" to a buyer. New settings: SUBPLZ_WEB_FREE_CREDITS_VERIFIED,
SUBPLZ_WEB_DAILY_FREE_CREDIT and SUBPLZ_WEB_BOOKS_PER_BONUS_CREDIT.
SUBPLZ_WEB_FREE_CREDITS_SIGNED_IN is gone.
