Test that the server consumes a Play purchase only after it commits the hours

No test failed when the credit broke one of its two money rules. The ledger row and the hours must go in one commit. The consume must come after that commit. A consume in play.credit before the commit, or a second commit before captions.add, passed all tests.

FakeGooglePlay now records each consume that comes before the commit of the ledger row, and the fixture google fails the test. A new test stops the database between the row and the hours. Then no row and no consume must stay, and the next send must give the hours.
