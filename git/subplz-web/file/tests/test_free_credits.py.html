1 """Free credits for conversions on the server.
2 
3 The rules under test:
4 
5   anonymous  one free credit, while the server takes conversions. A buyer
6              whose email came from Stripe is in this group until the email
7              is verified.
8   verified   an account that signed in with an emailed link gets two free
9              credits in all (a free credit that a device used before counts
10              toward the two), one more each day, and one more for every two
11              books that the server converted for it
12   daily      the daily credit does not carry over to the next day
13   order      a server job spends the daily credit, then a free credit, then
14              a bought one
15   refund     a failed job gives its credit back to the same kind
16   merge      a second device that signs in adds no free credits
17 """
