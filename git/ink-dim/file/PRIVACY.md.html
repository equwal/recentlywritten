1 # Privacy
2 
3 Ink Dim collects nothing.
4 
5 The app has no INTERNET permission. It cannot send data anywhere. There are no
6 advertisements and no analytics.
7 
8 ## What the permission does
9 
10 **Shizuku (moe.shizuku.manager.permission.API_V23).** It is the only permission
11 the app asks for. With it the app runs three shell commands on the device: it
12 reads the light of the panel, it writes the light of the panel, and it sets the
13 system brightness back.
14 
15 ## What the app stores
16 
17 The app stores one thing on the device: whether the light is down. That is how
18 the next tap knows which way to go. Android deletes it when you uninstall the
19 app.
