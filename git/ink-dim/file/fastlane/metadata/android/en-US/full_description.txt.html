1 Ink Dim sets the frontlight below the lowest level of the system.
2 
3 Tap the icon. The light goes to the lowest level the hardware still lights. Tap
4 it again. The light goes back to the level the system holds.
5 
6 The app has no screen. The icon is the whole app. The value holds across sleep,
7 until the system brightness is next set.
8 
9 The system will not set the frontlight below its own floor, so Ink Dim writes
10 the light of the panel straight. That file belongs to the system, so the app
11 needs a shell. Shizuku gives it one. Shizuku is a separate free app that you
12 start from the device through wireless debugging. There is no computer and no
13 root.
14 
15 There is a quick settings tile, "Extra dim". There are three intent actions, so
16 a key remapper can put the toggle on a hardware button:
17 dev.equwal.inkdim.TOGGLE, dev.equwal.inkdim.ON and dev.equwal.inkdim.OFF.
18 
19 A long press on the icon opens a settings screen. It shows the state and the
20 steps to set Shizuku up.
21 
22 Made for the Viwoods AiPaper readers. Other devices can have no light that the
23 app knows. Then the settings screen says so.
24 
25 Ink Dim collects nothing. It has no INTERNET permission, no advertisements and
26 no analytics.
