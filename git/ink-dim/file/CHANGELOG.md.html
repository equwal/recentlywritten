3 ## 0.1.4
4 
5 - The app names Shizuku in its package-visibility list. Without it, Android 11
6   and later hid Shizuku from the app, so the settings screen never offered the
7   permission step and the toggle could not run.
8 
9 ## 0.1.3
10 
11 - The app declares the Shizuku provider, so Shizuku can hand it the shell.
12 - The description says that the toggle may need `su`.
13 
14 ## 0.1.2
15 
16 - The signed APK has no dependency list for Google in it, so F-Droid can check that its own build is the same.
17 
18 ## 0.1.1
19 
20 - The APK is smaller: R8 removes the code that the app does not use.

22 ## 0.1.0
23 
24 First release.
25 
26 - Tap the icon to set the frontlight below the lowest level of the system.
27 - Tap the icon again to go back to the system level.
28 - A quick settings tile, "Extra dim", with the state.
29 - The intent actions `dev.equwal.inkdim.TOGGLE`, `dev.equwal.inkdim.ON` and
30   `dev.equwal.inkdim.OFF`.
31 - A "Settings" shortcut on a long press of the icon. It shows the state of
32   Shizuku and the steps to set it up.
