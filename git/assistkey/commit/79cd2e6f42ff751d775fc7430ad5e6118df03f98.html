Handle hidden power settings being unreadable, not just unwritable

Android 12 blocks non-system apps from reading settings keys annotated
@hide, and holding WRITE_SECURE_SETTINGS does not exempt you - reading
power_button_short_press threw SecurityException and took the main
screen down on first launch.

Every read now returns null on refusal, the UI says unknown instead of
inventing a value, and the two gesture switches became explicit On/Off
pickers because there is nothing to toggle away from when the current
value cannot be read. Writes are unaffected and still work.
