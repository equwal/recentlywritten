Sync on sbm.subread.space is free

The sbm-sync server made sync free for all accounts on 2026-09-22
(equwal/sbm-sync@f37b3a0). Three texts still described the old paid
plan. Now they say that sync on sbm.subread.space is free:

- README.md, in the Sync section.
- fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/full_description.txt, the store
  description for Google Play and F-Droid.
- play/listing.md, in the Pricing notes for Play Console. These notes
  also say that the app does not link to the payment pages of
  sbm.subread.space.

fdroid/com.equwal.sbm.yml gives no price, so it does not change.
