5 ## Upload
6 
7 - File: the Play bundle, built with `./gradlew bundleRelease -PplayStore=true`
8   (no Ko-fi link: Google Play does not allow links to payments outside
9   Google Play). Upload key: `Desktop\sbm-android-signing` (see its README).
10 - Use Play App Signing (the default).
11 - Personal developer accounts made after 13 November 2023 must run a closed
12   test with at least 12 testers for 14 days in a row before production.

26 ## App content
27 
28 - **Privacy policy:** https://sbm.subread.space/privacy
29 - **Ads:** no ads.
30 - **App access:** some functions need an account (sync). Give the review
31   account in `Desktop\sbm-android-signing\play-review-account.txt`, with
32   these steps: "Open the menu, tap Sign in to sync. The server is filled in.
33   Enter the email and the password, tap Sign in."
34 - **Content rating** (IARC questionnaire), category "Utility, Productivity,
35   Communication, or Other": no violence, no sexual content, no bad
36   language, no drugs, no gambling; users do not talk to other users or
37   share content with other users; no sharing of location; no purchases of
38   digital goods. Expected result: Everyone / PEGI 3.
39 - **Target audience:** 18 and over.
40 - **News app:** no. **Government app:** no. **Financial features:** none.
41   **Health:** no.
42 - **Data safety:**
43   - Does the app collect or share user data? Yes, it collects; it shares
44     nothing.
45   - Is all data encrypted in transit? Yes (HTTPS).
46   - Can users ask for deletion of their data? Yes:
47     https://sbm.subread.space/privacy#delete
48   - Personal info, email address: collected, optional, for account
49     management. Not shared.
50   - App activity, other user-generated content (the bookmarks): collected,
51     optional, for app functionality. Not shared.
52   - No location, no contacts, no device IDs, no crash logs, no analytics.
53   - Data is collected only when the user signs in to sync.
54 - **Account deletion:** users make accounts on the website, not in the app.
55   Deletion URL: https://sbm.subread.space/privacy#delete
