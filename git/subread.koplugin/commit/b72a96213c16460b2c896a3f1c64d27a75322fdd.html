Do not cover the narrated line with the controls

"Start read-along" opened the controls dialog at once. On a tablet the
dialog covered the line that the narrator read, and its clock and cue
stayed those of the time when it opened.

Start now shows a short notice only. The same menu entry opens the
controls when the read-along runs. Open controls are built again when
the cue changes, so the clock and the cue are current.

Tested on a Viwoods AiPaper with a Japanese book: the mark follows the
cues, and the page turns.
