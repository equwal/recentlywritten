14 Add voice typing: speak, and the words go where the cursor is (0.0.4-alpha)
15 
16 New action Typing &gt; Voice typing. The app has no speech engine and no network
17 access. It calls the Android SpeechRecognizer interface, so a speech
18 recognition app on the device does the listening. TextInsert puts the words
19 into the field that has input focus through the accessibility service, with a
20 clipboard paste as the fallback. It never writes into a password field.
21 
22 Android gives the microphone to the app in front only, and an accessibility
23 service does not count. The listening runs in DictationActivity, which is
24 see-through and cannot take focus or touches.
25 
26 The Viwoods voice prompt cannot be used. It uploads audio to the Viwoods cloud
27 and has no interface for other apps.
28 
29 Debug builds carry FakeRecognitionService and InsertReceiver, so the chain can
30 be tested with no voice. Tested on the reader: &quot;ab&quot; became
31 &quot;ab hello from the test bench&quot;.
32 
33 New permission: RECORD_AUDIO, requested at run time from the Voice typing
34 screen. Listing, privacy policy and declarations updated.
