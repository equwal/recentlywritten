126 <span class="a">+# Monetization models for Rebind</span>
127 <span class="a">+</span>
128 <span class="a">+## Recommendation</span>
129 <span class="a">+</span>
130 <span class="a">+Keep the free core for everyone. Sell Pro through two one-time unlocks, not a</span>
131 <span class="a">+subscription. On Google Play, sell the `play` build&#x27;s Pro unlock for **US$4.99**</span>
132 <span class="a">+(already coded). Off Play, sell the `full` build&#x27;s licence through Lemon</span>
133 <span class="a">+Squeezy for **US$9.99**, for e-ink readers that have no Play Store. Add a</span>
134 <span class="a">+no-gate tip link (Ko-fi or GitHub Sponsors) for people who want to give more.</span>
135 <span class="a">+This combination reaches paying users and non-paying users at the same time,</span>
136 <span class="a">+which matches the goal to help people improve their devices.</span>
137 <span class="a">+</span>
138 <span class="a">+## Model comparison</span>
139 <span class="a">+</span>
140 <span class="a">+| Model | Real example | User pays | Policy risk | Dev effort | Revenue potential | Goodwill / reach |</span>
141 <span class="a">+|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|</span>
142 <span class="a">+| Free + Play one-time Pro IAP | Button Mapper (~US$9.99), MacroDroid (~US$4-5) | Once, in the Play build | Low on Play; no reach off Play | Low, code exists | Modest, small niche | High, free core for all |</span>
143 <span class="a">+| Paid app, free trial off Play | Tasker (Play ~US$3.49, free 7-day trial APK) | Once, after trial | None, direct download | Low, `full` build exists | Modest, reaches non-Play devices | Medium, trial ends |</span>
144 <span class="a">+| Donations / tip jar | KOReader (~€47/week, Liberapay) | Any amount, or none | None, allowed everywhere | Very low, one link | Small, uneven | Very high, nothing locked |</span>
145 <span class="a">+| Open-core, paid extras | Key Mapper (open source, paid convenience) | For extras only | Low; F-Droid lists only the FOSS part | Medium, needs a clean split | Modest | Very high, source stays open |</span>
146 <span class="a">+| Pay-what-you-want / sponsorware | Common on itch.io, Gumroad | Any amount they pick | None | Low-medium, needs a store page | Variable, often low | Very high, ends up free |</span>
147 <span class="a">+</span>
148 <span class="a">+## Generous but paid</span>
149 <span class="a">+</span>
150 <span class="a">+Three ways to help people and still earn money:</span>
151 <span class="a">+</span>
152 <span class="a">+1. **Free forever, no trial timer:** basic remap of two buttons stays free on</span>
153 <span class="a">+   every device, always. This covers the most common hardware-hacking need.</span>
154 <span class="a">+2. **Free licence on request:** anyone who cannot pay gets a full Pro licence</span>
155 <span class="a">+   by email. Do not ask for proof. Trust the request.</span>
156 <span class="a">+3. **All e-ink profiles are free:** device profiles for Viwoods, Boox, and</span>
157 <span class="a">+   other e-ink readers stay free, because these readers are the project&#x27;s</span>
158 <span class="a">+   reason to exist. Pro sells only power features, such as multi-tap,</span>
159 <span class="a">+   combinations, menus and the full Power button. Settings export and import</span>
160 <span class="a">+   stay free (see the rules below).</span>
161 <span class="a">+</span>
162 <span class="a">+## Risks and rules</span>
163 <span class="a">+</span>
164 <span class="a">+- **Play payments policy:** the Play-distributed APK must use Play Billing</span>
165 <span class="a">+  for purchases used inside it. Do not add an external payment link inside</span>
166 <span class="a">+  the `play` build. Sell the Lemon Squeezy licence only in the `full` build.</span>
167 <span class="a">+- **No prohibited claims:** do not call the app a &quot;RAM booster&quot; or &quot;battery</span>
168 <span class="a">+  saver.&quot; These claims are false for a button remapper, and Play bans them.</span>
169 <span class="a">+- **Refunds:** Play handles its own refund window. For the Lemon Squeezy</span>
170 <span class="a">+  licence, publish one rule: refund within 14 days, no questions asked.</span>
171 <span class="a">+- **Never lock behind payment:** the control that turns off Power-button</span>
172 <span class="a">+  handling, and settings export and import. A user must always undo a bad</span>
173 <span class="a">+  remap and keep their own data.</span>
174 <span class="a">+</span>
175 <span class="a">+## Launch sequence and kill criteria</span>
176 <span class="a">+</span>
177 <span class="a">+1. Ship the free public beta now, on both builds. Collect the 12 testers for</span>
178 <span class="a">+   14 days that Play requires.</span>
179 <span class="a">+2. Open Play production: free core, plus the US$4.99 Pro one-time unlock.</span>
180 <span class="a">+3. At the same time, open the Lemon Squeezy US$9.99 licence for the `full`</span>
181 <span class="a">+   build. This reaches e-ink readers with no Play Store, the audience the</span>
182 <span class="a">+   owner most wants to help.</span>
183 <span class="a">+4. Add a tip link (Ko-fi or GitHub Sponsors) to the README of the public</span>
184 <span class="a">+   repository and to the Licence screen of the `full` build, with no feature</span>
185 <span class="a">+   gate attached. Do not put it in the `play` build.</span>
186 <span class="a">+</span>
187 <span class="a">+**Kill criteria:** fewer than 25 paid unlocks, counting Play and Lemon</span>
188 <span class="a">+Squeezy together, in the 60 days after Play production. If this happens, drop</span>
189 <span class="a">+the paid Pro tier and keep the free app plus the tip link only.</span>
