14 Fix: a Power press on a sleeping or locked device always wakes and unlocks it
15 
16 While the app manages the Power button, the firmware reaction to a short press
17 is off. A press on a sleeping or locked device then had only the firmware&#x27;s own
18 wake to rely on, and the press could also run the tap action (Back).
19 
20 KeyFilterService now treats such a press as a wake and never as a gesture. It
21 tracks SCREEN_OFF and SCREEN_ON, because the firmware can set isInteractive
22 before the press reaches this process. It also sends KEYCODE_WAKEUP and
23 `wm dismiss-keyguard` through the shell, so the wake does not depend on the
24 firmware. A press within one second after the screen comes on is the press that
25 woke it, and is ignored.
26 
27 Regression test on a device: tools/e2e/power-wake.sh.
