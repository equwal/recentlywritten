14 Fix voice typing refused with every grant in place, and the assistant setup
15 
16 Voice typing: the speech service of a normal app answered error 9 on
17 the Viwoods reader. The firmware does not count that service as in use,
18 so the while-in-use microphone grant of the speech app did not apply.
19 DictationActivity now falls back to the ACTION_RECOGNIZE_SPEECH screen
20 of the same app, takes the words from its result, and remembers the
21 route for that app. noHistory is gone from the activity, because an
22 activity with noHistory gets no result. DictationRouteTest holds the
23 error that caused the defect.
24 
25 Assistant setup: the request intent of the assistant role closes at
26 once, because Android has no dialog for that role. The app opens
27 ACTION_VOICE_INPUT_SETTINGS.
