Test the panel with the text of five languages

The overlay shows the lines of a subtitle file and cuts a word out of a line.
Both steps are sensitive to the script. Only English and Japanese had a test,
so a defect in another language was not found by a test.

Add a property test that writes and reads a subtitle file with lines of
English, Portuguese, Spanish, Russian and Japanese. The line must come back
byte for byte: the accents, the inverted marks, the guillemets and the
ideographic space.

Add cases to the word cutter test on the device for Portuguese, Spanish and
Russian. A word with an accent, and a word after an inverted mark or a
guillemet, must be one word.
