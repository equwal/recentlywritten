Look up a word when the finger lifts from it

A touch on a word pauses the player at once. Before, the player ran on
until the user pressed "Look up", and the line could go past.

When the finger lifts, the selection goes to the dictionary. A drag
before the lift selects more words. The "Look up" button is removed.
A touch beside the words does not pause the player.
