Add the play button, the see-through panel and the lines around

"Look up" pauses the player, so that the narration does not run on
while the user reads the dictionary. A play button on the panel starts
the player again, or pauses it.

A slider on the screen of the app sets how much of the player shows
through the panel. The text and the buttons stay as they are.

A setting shows the line before and the line after the line of now on
the panel, small and grey. The panel grows to fit them. A tap on a word
of those lines selects it too.

The follower reports the index of the line and the play state, not
the cue, so that the listener can find the lines around.
