Fix the faults that a review of the pop-up found

- A sender that gives the dictionary form (食べる) with a sentence that
  holds another form (食べた) got no bold and the whole text as the
  sentence. Scans.locate now finds the form of the text with the
  dictionary, and the card keeps the word of the sender.
- The sound of a word played after the pop-up closed, and prepare()
  blocked the UI thread. The player now prepares on its own thread and
  starts only while the pop-up is open.
- A long selection lost its lines after the fifth. The text now scrolls
  in a box of at most three tenths of the pop-up.
- A pop-up closed with no card now answers RESULT_CANCELED with
  "cancelled", as the README says. A picture added to the last card
  answers RESULT_OK.
- "+ Anki" before AnkiDroid allowed the app now asks for the permission
  again, in place of an error from AnkiDroid.
- A clip cut from the audio of a sender is left out when it is silence,
  the same as a clip of the capture.
- Sentences.around, unescape and stripTags had no user left. They are
  gone; the tests keep their cases with local helpers.
