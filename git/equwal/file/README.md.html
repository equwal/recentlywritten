40 ## SubRead
41 
42 Read along with audiobooks, and mine the words.
43 
44 * [subread.space](https://github.com/equwal/subread.space) - Read an `.srt` one line at a time with the space bar. Works with Yomitan, furigana, and pitch accent markup.
45 * [subplz-web](https://github.com/equwal/subplz-web) - The aligner behind subread.space. The subtitle text comes from your book, not from a transcript.
46 * [subread-android](https://github.com/equwal/subread-android) - SubRead on Android. Times an audiobook against its ebook on the device.
47 * [subread-extension](https://github.com/equwal/subread-extension) - Show your own `.srt` over a YouTube video, in time. Chrome and Firefox.
48 * [subread-overlay](https://github.com/equwal/subread-overlay) - Show `.srt` lines over any Android media player. Tap a word to look it up.
49 * [subread-dictionary](https://github.com/equwal/subread-dictionary) - Pop-up dictionary for Android. Reads Yomitan dictionaries, with local audio.
50 * [subread.koplugin](https://github.com/equwal/subread.koplugin) - KOReader plugin. The book follows the narration, from an `.srt` made by subread.space.
51 * [subrep-android](https://github.com/equwal/subrep-android) - Live captions of the phone's sound, made on the phone by Whisper, shared by link.
52 * [desktop-subtitle-replay](https://github.com/equwal/desktop-subtitle-replay) - Live subtitles for anything on your screen. Replay a sentence and mine it into Anki.
53 * [honjimaku](https://github.com/equwal/honjimaku) - The honjimaku.com server. Fork of [jimaku](https://github.com/Rapptz/jimaku).
54 * [kikiyomi](https://github.com/equwal/kikiyomi) - Audiobook player for Japanese learners, in the browser. Fork of [kikiyomi](https://github.com/rtr46/kikiyomi).
55 * [Hoshi-Reader-Android](https://github.com/equwal/Hoshi-Reader-Android) - Fork with a `subread` branch: audiobook read-along in the Hoshi Reader EPUB reader.
56 * [chimahon](https://github.com/equwal/chimahon) - Fork with a `readalong` branch for the Mihon immersion reader.
57 * [dickt.store](https://github.com/equwal/dickt.store) - Bring-your-own-key Yomitan dictionary converter.
58 * [vibeslop-dickt](https://github.com/equwal/vibeslop-dickt) - Make your own Yomitan dictionary in a few minutes.

64 * [rebind](https://github.com/equwal/rebind) - Remap volume keys, Power, page-turn buttons, and the Viwoods AI key. Tap, double tap, hold, and combinations. Free beta APK.

67 * [ink-recents](https://github.com/equwal/ink-recents) - Recent-apps switcher for e-ink. No animation. Swipe up to close.
68 * [ink-dim](https://github.com/equwal/ink-dim) - Set the Viwoods frontlight below the lowest system level.
69 * [ink-update](https://github.com/equwal/ink-update) - Tells you when Rebind, Ink Recents, or Ink Dim has a new version.
