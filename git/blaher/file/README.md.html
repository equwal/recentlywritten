1 Blaher
2 ---
3 
4 Say stuff with a template that has variables inside. It's like
5 cowsay. Plus it's a macro, which is neat.
6 
7 Examples
8 ---
9 
10 ```lisp
11 (with-output-to-string (bag) (blah bag "Hi {{person}}!" :person "Joe")); => "Hi Joe!"
12 
13 (blah t "Hi {{person}}! I am {{computer-assistant}}, here to help you, {{person}}, with all of your problems."
14      :person "Fred"
15      :computer-assistant "Eliza")
16 
17 ; => Hi Fred! I am Eliza, here to help you, Fred, with all of your problems.
18 ```
19 ```lisp
20 (blaher "Hi {{person}}! I am {{computer-assistant}}, here to help you, {{person}}, with all of your problems.")
21 ```
22 macro expands to:
23 ```lisp
24 (LAMBDA (#:STREAM.6790 &KEY ((:COMPUTER-ASSISTANT #:G6791))
25          ((:PERSON #:G6792)))
26   (PRINC "Hi " #:STREAM.6790)
27   (PRINC #:G6792 #:STREAM.6790)
28   (PRINC "! I am " #:STREAM.6790)
29   (PRINC #:G6791 #:STREAM.6790)
30   (PRINC ", here to help you, " #:STREAM.6790)
31   (PRINC #:G6792 #:STREAM.6790)
32   (PRINC ", with all of your problems." #:STREAM.6790))
33 ```
