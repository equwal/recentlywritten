1 # sbm for Firefox and Chrome
2 
3 Your [sbm](https://github.com/equwal/sbm) bookmarks in the browser.
4 
5 ![bm adds a page in the terminal; the add-on shows it at once and adds another, which bm then shows](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/equwal/sbm-sync/master/demo/sbm-demo.gif)
6 
7 ([MP4](https://github.com/equwal/sbm-sync/raw/master/demo/sbm-demo.mp4), 80 seconds.)
8 
9 - **Search as you type** in the popup (Alt+Shift+M), with the fuzzy match of
10   fzf. Enter opens the first match. Text that is no bookmark opens as an
11   address, or as a web search, as in bm.
12 - **Address bar:** type `bm`, a space, then words.
13 - **Add this page** with a description and tags.
14 - **Sync** with bm on your computers, the
15   [sbm app for Android](https://github.com/equwal/sbm-android) and your other
16   browsers, through an [sbm-sync](https://github.com/equwal/sbm-sync) server.
17 
18 Without an account, the bookmarks stay in the browser. With an account, the
19 add-on syncs when it starts, when you open the popup, after you add a
20 bookmark, and every 30 minutes.
21 
22 The bookmarks are an sbm file: one bookmark per line,
23 `URL<tab>description<tab>tags`.
24 
25 ## Sync
26 
27 The add-on uses https://sbm.subread.space unless you give another server in
28 the options. Create an account there: it is free for 30 days, then $3 a month
29 or $30 a year. Or [run your own server](https://github.com/equwal/sbm-sync):
30 it is free software.
31 
32 ## Permissions
33 
34 - `storage`: keep the bookmarks and the sign-in token.
35 - `activeTab`: read the address and the title of the page when you click
36   "Add this page".
37 - `alarms`: sync every 30 minutes.
38 - Access to sbm.subread.space, or to the server that you choose: sync.
39 
40 Firefox also asks you, at sign-in, to let the add-on send your bookmarks,
41 your email address and your password to the server. The
42 [privacy policy](https://sbm.subread.space/privacy) tells what the server
43 keeps.
44 
45 ## Develop
46 
47 The add-on needs no build: load this directory as an unpacked extension
48 (Chrome: chrome://extensions, developer mode; Firefox: about:debugging).
49 
50     npm install
51     npm test          # property tests of the file format and the search
52     npm run lint      # the checks of addons.mozilla.org
53     npm run build     # the zip for the stores, in web-ext-artifacts/
