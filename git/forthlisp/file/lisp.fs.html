lisp.fs (34675 bytes)

1 \ lisp.fs -- an R7RS-small subset Scheme in Forth (our own kernel) for the STM32F446 (128 KiB RAM).
2 \ Values are 32-bit. Low 3 bits: xx1 fixnum (31-bit signed), 000 pair (0 is '()),
3 \ 010 symbol, 100 immediate (#f #t, unspecified, unbound, box tags, special-form markers,
4 \ characters), 110 procedure or box.
5 \ Pairs, symbols and procedures are 8-byte cells in `heap`: car word, cdr word.
6 \ Symbol: car = fixnum offset of its counted name in `names`, cdr = global value.
7 \ Procedure: car = fixnum primitive number, or a closure's parameters; closure cdr = (body . env).
8 \ Box: a 110 cell whose car is a box tag. Strings, vectors and bytevectors keep their contents
9 \ in a contiguous blob in `bheap`; the box's cdr holds the blob's data address. A blob header
10 \ names its one owner box, so the GC can slide live blobs down and fix the owner's cdr.
11 \ Rule: never hold a blob address across an allocation (cons, newbox); re-fetch it from the box.
12 \ A character is an immediate: (code+1) << 12 | 4.
13 \ An environment is an alist of (symbol . value) bindings in front of the globals.
14 \ GC: mark-sweep, run when cons finds the free list empty. Roots: the symbol list, plus
15 \ every word on the data and return stacks that looks like a heap reference (conservative).
