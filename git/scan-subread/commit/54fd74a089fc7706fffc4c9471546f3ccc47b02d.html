Grow the box over CJK punctuation in its own OCR word

On the sample-jpn scan the box of a line stopped before the 。 at the
end of the cue. Tesseract gave that 。 a word of its own, and the box
grew only over tokens with the same word id. CJK has no spaces, so its
OCR words do not show where a word ends. After a CJK character the box
now grows over the punctuation next to the span on the same line,
whatever its word id: an opening mark before the span, another mark
after it. After a letter that is not CJK, the rule of the same word
stays.
