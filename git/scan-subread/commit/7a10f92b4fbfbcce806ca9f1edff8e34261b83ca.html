Hold the follow after a page turn by the user

While the audio played, a page turn by hand lasted only to the next
cue: the follow turned the page back 0.8 seconds later. Now a page turn
by the user (an arrow, a key, a swipe or the page jump) holds the
follow. While it is held, the page does not turn by itself. The mark
still shows when the cue of now is on the page shown. A Follow button
shows in the top bar, and the strip says "Follow paused".

The hold ends when the user taps Follow, when the audio reaches the
page shown, when the user moves the audio from this app (the cue list,
"move the audio to this page"), or when the player jumps. A new
alignment after a page is read is no seek: it has its own event, and
it does not end the hold. So cueMoved is not necessary any more.

follow() in follower.ts is now a function of the state: at each step
it compares the page of the audio with the page shown. It gets the
event (tick, realign, seek, follow) and the hold, and it gives the new
hold. Property tests show that a held follow never turns a page
without a seek or the Follow button, and that the hold ends when the
audio is on the page shown. lastCueAt (subtitles.ts) gives the cue of
now, also in the silence after a cue.
