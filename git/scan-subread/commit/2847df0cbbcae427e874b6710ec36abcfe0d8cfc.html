Extend the cue box to the edges of its words

The alignment drops punctuation and can drop a noisy letter at the
edge of a cue. The box of a line then stopped in a word, and the comma
after "hills" was outside. lineBoxes now grows the box of each line to
the edges of its first and its last word. In a CJK word only punctuation
joins, because Japanese OCR can give one word for a whole line. Each box
also gets 2 CSS pixels of padding, and the page clips it.
