Open the last book at its page with its subtitles

On the phone, Back, a force stop, or Android stopping the reader while
the user was in the dictionary lost everything: after the relaunch the
reader showed "No PDF" and no cues.

- Each open keeps the PDF as the last book in IndexedDB. At start the
  reader opens the last book at its page, with its subtitles, without a
  picker. The start card shows only when there is no last book.
- The page goes to the meta data of the book 1 s after it changes, and
  at once when the page is hidden or another book opens.
- Each subtitle file and each SubRead result goes to the meta data of
  the open book.
- Subtitles belong to a book. Another book loads its own subtitles, or
  has none: the cue list, the mark, the .srt row and "audio to page"
  clear. Subtitles that were loaded while no book was open stay, and
  are kept for the book that opens next (subtitlesOnOpen).
- A picked book shows its first page and starts the reading at once.
  Its meta data applies when it comes. The first open after the upgrade
  from the dictionary build waited about 30 s with no text. When the
  database is not open after 2 s, the strip says "Updating the page
  cache..." and the start card shows.
- When the copy of the last book cannot be read or opened, the reader
  forgets it and shows the start card.

getSrt and putSrt are gone. clearLastBook and dbReady are new, with
tests.
