+# Privacy policy of Scan SubRead
+
+Scan SubRead has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The
+developer gets no data from the app.
+
+## Data on the device
+
+The app keeps these items in its own storage on the device:
+
+- a copy of the last book that you opened,
+- the text of the pages that it read,
+- the page, the subtitles and the settings of each book.
+
+Uninstalling the app deletes them. "Clear all pages" in the menu deletes
+the pages.
+
+## Data that goes to other apps on the device
+
+- The text that you tap goes to the dictionary app that you choose.
+- A word and its sentence go to SubRead Anki when you make a card.
+- The text of the book goes to SubRead when you make subtitles.
+- The app asks SubRead Overlay for the position of the audiobook player.
+
+These apps are on your device. Scan SubRead sends nothing to a server
+for them.
+
+## Network
+
+The first OCR run of a language downloads the language data of Tesseract
+from `cdn.jsdelivr.net`. The request sends no data about you or your
+books. The CDN sees the IP address of the device, as each web server
+does. The privacy policy of jsDelivr applies to it:
+https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net
+
+The app makes no other network request.
+
+## Contact
+
+Open an issue at https://github.com/equwal/scan-subread/issues.

+Scan SubRead is a PDF reader that follows the audiobook. It works with scans and with text PDFs. It is made for people who read and listen in a language that they learn.
+
+Load a PDF and the subtitle file (.srt) of its audiobook. The app reads the text of each page, aligns each subtitle line to it, and follows the player: it marks the line that the narrator reads and turns the page.
+
+<b>What it does</b>
+<ul>
+<li>Scans: OCR reads each page in the app, also Japanese, horizontal and vertical. Text PDFs: the app reads the text layer.</li>
+<li>Tap a word: the text from that word to the end of the line goes to the dictionary of your choice. The player pauses while the dictionary is open.</li>
+<li>Press a word for half a second: SubRead Anki makes a card with its sentence.</li>
+<li>"Make subtitles with SubRead": pick the audiobook, and SubRead makes the .srt from the audio and the text of the book.</li>
+<li>The app keeps the last book, its page and its subtitles, and opens them at the next start.</li>
+<li>After a page turn by hand, the page stops following. The Follow button brings it back.</li>
+</ul>
+
+<b>The SubRead suite</b>
+<ul>
+<li>SubRead Overlay reads the position of the audiobook player and gives it to this app.</li>
+<li>SubRead Dictionary, or another app in the text selection menu, looks up the words.</li>
+<li>SubRead makes the subtitle file.</li>
+<li>SubRead Anki makes the cards.</li>
+</ul>
+The start card shows which of these apps are installed.
+
+<b>Network</b>
+The first OCR run of a language downloads the language data of Tesseract from cdn.jsdelivr.net. The app keeps it after that. Nothing else leaves the device.
