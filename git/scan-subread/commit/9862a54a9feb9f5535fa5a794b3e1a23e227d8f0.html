Add the SubRead plugin, the clock sources and the page cache

SubReadPlugin.java is the bridge to the suite: the player through the content provider of SubRead Overlay (release and debug authorities), the dictionary through ACTION_PROCESS_TEXT (the call resolves when the dictionary closes), the subtitle maker through the intent API of SubRead, the audio picker and the share sheet. The manifest declares the queries that Android 11 needs.

clock-source.ts gives the reader one interface over two time sources: OverlayClock polls the overlay every 2 s (5 s after an error), computes the position between two reads with the play clock, marks a jump over 3 s as a seek, and skips one read after its own play, pause or seek. LocalAudioClock wraps the audio element on the web.

token-cache.ts keeps the tokens of each page and the subtitles of each book in IndexedDB. The lookup text now has a space between words.
