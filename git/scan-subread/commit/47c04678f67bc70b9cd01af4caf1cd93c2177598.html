Reject a player command that the player did not do

OverlayClock.play, pause and seek resolved when the player answered an error line (error=no_player, error=no_overlay, error=no_notification_access). The UI then said "Audio moved to 00:17." when the audio did not move. The clock now sets the error state and then rejects with a PlayerError. The reason field and the message hold the reason, for example "no_player".

LocalAudioClock.play and seek now reject with PlayerError("no_player") when no audio is loaded. Before, seek resolved and play failed with the error of the audio element.
