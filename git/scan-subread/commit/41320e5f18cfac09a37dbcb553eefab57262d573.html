Say a player problem once in the strip

On the phone, play without notification access gave the same sentence
two times: in the player part and in the event part. A PlayerError for
the problem of the last player state now shows only in the player part
while a book is open (shownInPlayerPart). Before a book is open the
player part is hidden, so the event part still says it.
