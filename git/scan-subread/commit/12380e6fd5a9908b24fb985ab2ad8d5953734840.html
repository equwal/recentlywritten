Tell when SubRead waits for pages that could not be read

"Make subtitles with SubRead" waits until all pages of the book are
read. startMakeWhenRead ran only after a page that was read. After "OCR
could not start..." or "2 pages could not be read.", the menu still
said "Reading pages 38/40 first...", and SubRead never started.

readBook now calls startMakeWhenRead also when the reading ends, and
when it starts. waitMessage (messages.ts) gives "Reading pages N/M
first..." while the reading runs. After the reading it gives "SubRead
waits for 2 pages that could not be read." with Retry in the menu.
Retry reads those pages, and startMakeWhenRead then starts SubRead
after the last one.
