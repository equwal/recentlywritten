Move the Follow button over the page, out of the top bar

On the phone the Follow button was the last item of the top bar. When
it showed, the page label got 65 px narrower, and each button right of
the label moved left (Next page from x 295 to 230, "audio to this page"
from 371 to 306). A quick second tap on Next page then pressed "audio to
this page" and moved the audio, and a tap on the usual place of play
pressed Follow.

The Follow button now floats over the lower right corner of the page,
48 px high. The buttons of the top bar stay where they are: at 360 CSS
px, Next page and "audio to this page" keep x 244 and 320 when Follow
shows. "Follow paused" stays in the strip.
