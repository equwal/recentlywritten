Describe the strip, the page turns and the follow hold in the README

The README told of Prev and Next, of a status line in the menu, and of
a lookup from the tapped character. It now tells of the start card, the
status strip, the icons of the top bar, the keys, the swipe, the page
jump and the reading direction. It tells how the follow holds after a
page turn by hand, and how a cue at a page break turns the page. The
lookup text starts at the word in Latin script and skips punctuation.
The layout lists the new modules.
