Start a lookup at the word, and skip punctuation

A tap inside an English word sent the rest of the line from the tapped letter, for example "rning sun rose over the quiet hills,". The dictionary looks up the longest match at the start of the text, so it looked up "rning". A tap on a character that is not CJK now starts the lookup at the first letter of its word. The search for the start of the word stops at a CJK character, because Japanese OCR can give one word for a whole line. A CJK character still starts the lookup where the tap lands.

Punctuation and symbols at the start of the lookup text are now skipped. When only punctuation is left on the line, the lookup text is empty. Before, a tap on the last "。" of a line sent "。" and the next line.

The next line now follows a short line only when the line does not end a sentence (。．！？.!?」』). Before, "無い。" took the next line.
