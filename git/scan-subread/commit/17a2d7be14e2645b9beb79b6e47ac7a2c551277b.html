Cut off the OCR start with its own error, not AbortSignal.reason

abort(reason) and AbortSignal.reason came in Chrome 98, and the app
runs on WebView 91 and later (the SIMD core of tesseract.js). On an
older WebView, a start that took too long rejected with undefined, not
with OcrStartError. The strip then said "40 pages could not be read."
in place of "OCR could not start".

createOcrJob now rejects the start with the error that it keeps, and
also aborts the signal of the start with it. A test makes abort() drop
its reason, as an older WebView does.
