Make each status message readable on a phone

The status strip is one line, so the page does not move. On the phone
a long event message was cut to "No subtitle li..." next to "Allow
notification acces..." and the "Open SubRead Overlay" button. The full
text was in the title attribute, and a phone cannot hover.

- While an event shows, it takes the place of the player text. The
  link or the button of the player stays.
- A tap on the strip, not on a link or a button in it, shows the full
  texts of the strip in a panel (#strip-full) over the top of the page.
  The panel has a fixed position, so the page does not move. The next
  tap closes it, and else it closes after 6 s, the time of an event.

stripTexts (messages.ts) gives the texts of the panel: the title of
each part that shows, else its text.
