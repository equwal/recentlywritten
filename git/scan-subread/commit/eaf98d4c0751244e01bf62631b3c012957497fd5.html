Make the buttons of the top bar 48 px

The buttons of the top bar were 36 x 36 CSS px with 2 px between them,
under the 48 dp touch target of the Android guideline. They are now
48 x 48 px with 4 px between them, and the bar is 52 px high. With the
Follow button out of the bar, the bar stays in one row at 360 CSS px:
five buttons, and 92 px for the page label. The page jump has the same
height.
