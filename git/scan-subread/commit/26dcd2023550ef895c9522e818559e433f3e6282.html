Keep the status strip one line high, so the page does not move

On the phone a long event message wrapped in the strip and pushed the
page down 31.5 CSS px, and the page went up again when the message
faded 6.6 s later. The strip is now one line of a fixed height (32 px).
A long text ends with an ellipsis, and the title attribute of its part
holds the full text. Links, buttons and "Follow paused" do not shrink,
and the player text shrinks last. At 360 CSS px the top of the page
stays at 84 px before, during and after a long message.
