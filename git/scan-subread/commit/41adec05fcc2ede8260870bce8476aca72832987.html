Mark only one cue in the list, and scroll a new page after a resize

After a new PDF, the cue list could keep the mark of the old cue,
because the list removed the mark by the index of the cue before. The
list now removes the mark from the item that has it.

The page view decided "new page" when the render started. A resize
during a page turn then showed the new page without a scroll to its
mark or its top. The view now decides when the render ends, from the
page on the canvas.
