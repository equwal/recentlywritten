Keep the book text out of the saved state of the reader

While SubRead makes the subtitles, Capacitor keeps the options of the
makeSubtitles call in the saved state of the activity, two times: once
from the bridge and once from the plugin. The saved state goes to the
system when SubRead covers the reader, and it has a limit of about
1 MB. A book text of about 250 000 characters or more is over that
limit, so Android stopped the reader with TransactionTooLargeException.

SubRead reads the book text from the file book.txt. The call now
removes the book text from its options after it writes that file.
