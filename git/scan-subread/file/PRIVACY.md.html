1 # Privacy policy of Scan SubRead
2 
3 Scan SubRead has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The
4 developer gets no data from the app.
5 
6 ## Data on the device
7 
8 The app keeps these items in its own storage on the device:
9 
10 - a copy of the last book that you opened,
11 - the text of the pages that it read,
12 - the page, the subtitles and the settings of each book.
13 
14 Uninstalling the app deletes them. "Clear all pages" in the menu deletes
15 the pages.
16 
17 ## Data that goes to other apps on the device
18 
19 - The text that you tap goes to the dictionary app that you choose.
20 - A word and its sentence go to SubRead Anki when you make a card.
21 - The text of the book goes to SubRead when you make subtitles.
22 - The app asks SubRead Overlay for the position of the audiobook player.
23 
24 These apps are on your device. Scan SubRead sends nothing to a server
25 for them.
26 
27 ## Network
28 
29 The first OCR run of a language downloads the language data of Tesseract
30 from `cdn.jsdelivr.net`. The request sends no data about you or your
31 books. The CDN sees the IP address of the device, as each web server
32 does. The privacy policy of jsDelivr applies to it:
33 https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net
34 
35 The app makes no other network request.
