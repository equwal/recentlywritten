1 Scan SubRead is a PDF reader that follows the audiobook. It works with scans and with text PDFs. It is made for people who read and listen in a language that they learn.
2 
3 Load a PDF and the subtitle file (.srt) of its audiobook. The app reads the text of each page, aligns each subtitle line to it, and follows the player: it marks the line that the narrator reads and turns the page.
4 
5 <b>What it does</b>
6 <ul>
7 <li>Scans: OCR reads each page in the app, also Japanese, horizontal and vertical. Text PDFs: the app reads the text layer.</li>
8 <li>Tap a word: the text from that word to the end of the line goes to the dictionary of your choice. The player pauses while the dictionary is open.</li>
9 <li>Press a word for half a second: SubRead Anki makes a card with its sentence.</li>
10 <li>"Make subtitles with SubRead": pick the audiobook, and SubRead makes the .srt from the audio and the text of the book.</li>
11 <li>The app keeps the last book, its page and its subtitles, and opens them at the next start.</li>
12 <li>After a page turn by hand, the page stops following. The Follow button brings it back.</li>
13 </ul>
14 
15 <b>The SubRead suite</b>
16 <ul>
17 <li>SubRead Overlay reads the position of the audiobook player and gives it to this app.</li>
18 <li>SubRead Dictionary, or another app in the text selection menu, looks up the words.</li>
19 <li>SubRead makes the subtitle file.</li>
20 <li>SubRead Anki makes the cards.</li>
21 </ul>
22 The start card shows which of these apps are installed.
23 
24 <b>Network</b>
25 The first OCR run of a language downloads the language data of Tesseract from cdn.jsdelivr.net. The app keeps it after that. Nothing else leaves the device.
