1 # Subrep for Android
2 
3 [Subrep](https://honjimaku.com/subrep/) on a phone: live captions of the
4 sound of the device, made on the device by Whisper, shared by link. The
5 captions on the phone are free. Cloud captions, from a larger model, cost
6 hours that you buy on subread.space.
7 
8 The app captures the sound of the apps on the phone (a video, a game, a
9 browser) or the microphone. Whisper, on the phone, turns it into captions.
10 The captions go to your share link on `honjimaku.com`, where the viewers read
11 along and mine with Yomitan, and to
12 [SubRead Overlay](https://github.com/equwal/subread-overlay) over the player,
13 where a tap on a word opens the dictionary. With captions on the phone, no
14 sound leaves the phone.

24 ## How to use it
25 
26 1. Choose where the captions are made: on this phone (free), or in the
27    cloud. For the cloud, buy hours with a button under the choice, then come
28    back to the app. The cloud needs no model: go to step 3.
29 2. Choose the speech model and press "Download". `base` (82 MB) keeps up on
30    most phones. `small` (264 MB) reads better and comes late. `tiny` is for
31    an old phone.
32 3. Enter the language: `ja`, `en`, `ru`, ... or `auto`. A set language is
33    faster and more exact.
34 4. Choose the sound: the apps on this device (Android 10 or later), or the
35    microphone.
36 5. Press "Start the captions". Android asks for the capture of the screen
37    ("Share entire screen"), and once for the microphone permission. That
38    permission covers the sound of the apps too.
39 6. Start the video. The captions come to the app, to the link and to the
40    overlay. The notification has a "Stop" button.
41 
42 The share link stays the same across restarts. "New link" makes another one,
43 and the old one stops.
44 
45 ## Cloud captions
46 
47 The cloud uses Whisper large-v3 turbo on DeepInfra, through subread.space. It is
48 more exact than the models that a phone can run, and it uses less battery.
49 
50 | Pack | Price |
51 |---|---|
52 | 20 hours | $4.99 |
53 | 80 hours | $16.99 |
54 | 200 hours | $39.00 |
55 
56 Each piece of speech costs its length, rounded up to the next second.
57 Silence costs nothing: the app sends only speech. The hours do not expire.
58 
59 The hours belong to the account of this app on subread.space. The screen
60 shows its id (`acct_...`). If you install the app again, the app gets a new
61 account: write to the contact address on subread.space with the old id and
62 the receipt, and we move the hours.
63 
64 What goes where: each piece of speech goes to subread.space, which gives it
65 to DeepInfra for the text. subread.space keeps neither the sound nor the
66 text. DeepInfra says that it does not store them ([data privacy](https://docs.deepinfra.com/account/data-privacy)).
67 The app sends nothing while the phone makes the captions.
68 
69 ## What the phone cannot capture
70 
71 - An app that refuses the capture of its sound: a video app with DRM, and
72   some music apps. Android gives silence. Use the microphone.
73 - Sound from another device over Bluetooth. A phone is a Bluetooth source,
74   not a speaker.
75 
76 ## How it works
77 
78 `CaptureService` reads 16 kHz mono PCM from `AudioRecord` (with an
79 `AudioPlaybackCaptureConfiguration` for the apps, or the microphone).
80 `Segmenter` cuts it into pieces at pauses, with the rules of the desktop
81 app: a block of 32 ms is speech above the noise floor times 3, a pause of
82 0.65 s closes a piece, 11 s is the most. `Engine` gives each piece to a
83 `Recognizer` on one thread: whisper.cpp (`Whisper`), or the cloud
84 (`CloudRecognizer`, which posts 16-bit PCM to
85 `https://subread.space/api/captions/transcribe`). Whisper runs with the encoder window cut to the length of the
86 piece, and drops the invented lines that `Hallucinations` lists. Each piece
87 is one final caption.
88 
89 The relay protocol is that of `subrep.share`: a websocket to
90 `wss://honjimaku.com/subrep/pub/<room>`, a hello with the room secret, then
91 `{"type":"final","text":...,"tr":"","ts":...}` for each caption.
92 
93 The overlay protocol is the `line` method of the content provider
94 `content://space.subread.overlay.player`.
95 
96 `adb shell dumpsys activity service com.honjimaku.subrep/.CaptureService`
97 prints the state, for a bug report.

99 ## Build
100 
101 whisper.cpp is a submodule:
102 
103 ```
104 git submodule update --init
105 ./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest :app:assembleDebug
106 ```
107 
108 The native library is built for 64-bit ARM with ARMv8.2 half-precision and
109 dot-product instructions (each phone since 2018). NDK 29 and CMake 3.31.6
110 from the Android SDK.
111 
112 For a release, set `SUBREP_KEYSTORE_FILE`, `SUBREP_KEYSTORE_PASSWORD` and
113 `SUBREP_KEY_ALIAS` (default `subrep`), then `./gradlew :app:assembleRelease`.
