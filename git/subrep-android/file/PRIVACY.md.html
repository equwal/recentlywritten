1 # Privacy policy of Subrep for Android
2 
3 This policy is for the Android app Subrep (package `com.honjimaku.subrep`):
4 the build on Google Play, and the builds on GitHub and F-Droid. Contact:
5 support@honjimaku.com.
6 
7 ## Captions on the phone
8 
9 When Whisper on the phone makes the captions, the sound stays on the phone.
10 The app sends no sound.
11 
12 ## Data that leaves the phone
13 
14 - **Captions, when "Share the captions by link" is on.** The app sends each
15   caption line to the relay of honjimaku.com. The relay gives the lines to the
16   people who open your link. It keeps only the last 40 lines of each link, in
17   memory, for new viewers. Turn off the share to stop it, or press "New link"
18   to stop the old link.
19 - **Speech, only when you choose the cloud captions.** The app sends each
20   piece of speech to subread.space, which gives it to DeepInfra for the text.
21   subread.space keeps neither the sound nor the text. DeepInfra says that it
22   does not store them
23   ([data privacy](https://docs.deepinfra.com/account/data-privacy)).
24   subread.space takes the length of each piece from your hours.
25 - **An account.** When you choose the cloud captions, the app asks
26   subread.space for the hours of your account. The first time that you choose
27   them, subread.space makes the account. With the captions on the phone, the
28   app does not contact subread.space. The account has a random id (`acct_...`)
29   and a random device token.
30   It holds your hours. It has no name, email address or phone number.
31 - **Purchases.** In the Google Play build, Google Play takes the payment. The
32   app sends the purchase token to subread.space. subread.space asks Google Play
33   if the purchase is paid, and then adds the hours. It keeps the product, the
34   hours, the Google Play order number, the time and a hash of the token. If
35   Google Play refunds a purchase, subread.space takes back its hours. In the
36   GitHub and F-Droid builds, Stripe takes the payment on subread.space (see
37   the [terms of subread.space](https://subread.space/terms.html)).
38 - **The speech model.** The app downloads the model from Hugging Face.
39   Hugging Face sees the IP address of the phone, as for each download.
40 
41 ## What the app does not do
42 
43 The app shows no ads. It has no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell
44 data. We give data only to the services above, for the work above: DeepInfra
45 for the cloud text, Google Play or Stripe for the payment.
46 
47 ## Permissions
48 
49 - Screen capture and the microphone permission: for the sound of other apps,
50   or of the microphone. Android asks each time that the captions start, and
51   shows a notification while the capture runs.
52 - Internet: for the model download, the share link and the cloud captions.
53 - Notifications: for the notification with the "Stop" button.
54 
55 ## Delete your data
56 
57 Write to support@honjimaku.com with the account id that the app shows. We
58 delete the account and its hours. We keep only the purchase records that the
59 law tells us to keep. To delete the data on the phone, uninstall the app.
60
