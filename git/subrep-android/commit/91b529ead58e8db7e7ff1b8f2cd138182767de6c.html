Cut a loop of a phrase in a caption

The loop cut only saw a piece of up to 6 characters. Whisper base looped
on a Japanese phrase of 9 characters, live on Android 11, and the caption
kept six repeats of it. The cut now sees a piece of up to 40 characters.
A short piece keeps three repeats. A phrase keeps one. The cut runs until
nothing changes, so a loop that a cut uncovers is cut too.

The test holds the caption that showed the defect.
