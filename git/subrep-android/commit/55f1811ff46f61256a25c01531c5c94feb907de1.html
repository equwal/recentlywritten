Turn on R8, build the speech library the same on each machine, add the F-Droid listing, and set the version to 0.2.1

F-Droid asks for R8 and for a reproducible build. The ggml sources put their file paths in the library (__FILE__), and ggml records the git state of the checkout. CMakeLists.txt now maps the paths of the checkout and of the NDK to fixed names, writes / on Windows too, and turns off the git lookup. The fastlane folder gets the title, the descriptions, the icon and the changelog. The recipe names the signed APK on GitHub (Binaries) and its key, and it marks the share link and the cloud captions as NonFreeNet.

+# Reproducible build: F-Droid builds this library again, on Linux and at another
+# path, and compares it with the signed APK. The maps remove the paths of the
+# build machine from the __FILE__ strings of ggml. -ffile-reproducible writes "/"
+# in them on Windows too. An empty GIT_EXE stops ggml from recording the git state
+# of the checkout, which the F-Droid scanner changes.
+get_filename_component(SUBREP_ROOT "${CMAKE_CURRENT_SOURCE_DIR}/../../../.." ABSOLUTE)
+get_filename_component(SUBREP_TOOLCHAIN_BIN "${CMAKE_C_COMPILER}" DIRECTORY)
+get_filename_component(SUBREP_TOOLCHAIN_ROOT "${SUBREP_TOOLCHAIN_BIN}" DIRECTORY)
+add_compile_options(
+    -ffile-reproducible
+    "-ffile-prefix-map=${SUBREP_ROOT}=."
+    "-ffile-prefix-map=${SUBREP_TOOLCHAIN_ROOT}=toolchain")

+Subrep makes live captions of the sound of your device. Whisper makes them on the phone, and a share link gives them to other people.
+
+<b>How it works</b>
+<ul>
+<li>The app captures the sound of the apps on the phone (a video, a game, a browser; Android 10 or later) or the microphone.</li>
+<li>Whisper, on the phone, turns the sound into captions. No sound leaves the phone.</li>
+<li>The captions go to your share link on honjimaku.com, where viewers read along, and to SubRead Overlay over the player, where a tap on a word opens the dictionary.</li>
+</ul>
+
+<b>Speech models</b>
+The app downloads the model that you choose from Hugging Face (ggerganov/whisper.cpp): tiny, base (82 MB) or small (264 MB). Base keeps up on most phones.
+
+<b>Cloud captions (optional)</b>
+Cloud captions use Whisper large-v3 turbo on DeepInfra, through subread.space. They cost hours that you buy on subread.space. The app sends only speech, and only when you choose the cloud.
+
+<b>Permissions</b>
+<ul>
+<li>Screen capture and the microphone permission: for the sound of other apps. Android asks each time the captions start.</li>
+<li>Internet: for the model download, the share link and the cloud captions.</li>
+<li>Notifications: for the notification with the "Stop" button.</li>
+</ul>
