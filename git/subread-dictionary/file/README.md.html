1 # SubRead Dictionary
2 
3 A pop-up dictionary for Android that reads Yomitan dictionaries. Select a text
4 in any app and choose "SubRead Dictionary" in the text selection menu, share a
5 text to it, or send a word from
6 [SubRead Overlay](https://github.com/equwal/subread-overlay). The pop-up
7 opens over the app and shows each term at the start of the text, the longest
8 first, with the reading, the pitch accent, the frequency, and a button for
9 the audio. A tap on a character of the text looks up from there.
10 
11 Nothing leaves the device, except when a remote audio source is on.
12 
13 ## How to use it
14 
15 1. Import a dictionary: a Yomitan `.zip` (JMdict, Jitendex, a pitch accent
16    dictionary, a frequency list). The app reads format 3. The order of the
17    dictionaries in the settings is the order in the pop-up.
18 2. Local audio, optional: choose the `android.db` of the
19    [Local Audio Server for Yomitan](https://github.com/yomidevs/local-audio-yomichan),
20    the same file that AnkiConnect Android and Hoshi Reader use. The app copies
21    it into its folder. Or push it there with `adb`; the settings show the
22    folder. The order of the sources is a setting: `jpod, jpod_alternate,
23    nhk16, shinmeikai8, forvo`.
24 3. Remote audio, optional and off by default: one URL per line, with `{term}`
25    and `{reading}`. A URL can answer with an audio file (JapanesePod101 is
26    the default line), or with the JSON list of a Local Audio Server on the
27    network: `http://192.168.1.2:5050/?term={term}&reading={reading}`. The
28    remote sources come after the local ones.
29 
30 In the pop-up, "Play" plays the first source that has the word. A long press
31 on "Play" lists every source.
32 
33 ## How it works
34 
35 `:core` is plain Kotlin, with no Android in it:
36 
37 - `YomitanZip` reads the zip as a stream, one term at a time.
38 - `Deinflector` has the Japanese rules of Yomitan: 食べさせられた → 食べる,
39   with the word class of each step checked against the rules of the term.
40 - `Lookup` tries each prefix of the text, the longest first, and each
41   dictionary form of it, in one query.
42 - `Glossary` turns the structured content of a term into the simple HTML
43   that a `TextView` shows.
44 
45 `:app` has the SQLite store of the terms, the settings screen, the pop-up and
46 the audio.
47 
48 ## For other apps
49 
50 Send a text with any of these; the pop-up opens over your app:
51 
52 - `Intent.ACTION_PROCESS_TEXT` with `EXTRA_PROCESS_TEXT` (the text selection menu).
53 - `Intent.ACTION_SEND`, `text/plain`, with `EXTRA_TEXT` (the share sheet).
54 - The action `space.subread.dictionary.LOOKUP` with `EXTRA_TEXT`.
55 
56 ## Build
57 
58 ```
59 ./gradlew :core:test :app:assembleDebug
60 ./gradlew :app:connectedDebugAndroidTest   # the SQLite store, on a device
61 ```
62 
63 The device test installs the debug app again and removes its data: the
64 imported dictionaries of the debug build are gone after it.
65 
66 `-PplayStore=true` leaves the Ko-fi link out of the build for Google Play.
