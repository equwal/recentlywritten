Digest: send no old items of a feed that is followed later

The digest noted the items of the feeds only at its first run. A feed
that the account followed after that brought all its old items into the
next digest. Example: 112 feeds followed at one time would give a digest
of more than 1,500 items.

Now the job notes the first items that it finds of a new feed, and sends
none of them. The new file known has a line for each noted feed: the
URL, a tab and the item file. A feed that the account leaves, or that
gets another item file, is a new feed again. Without that file, as after
this update, all followed feeds are known, so no waiting item is lost.
