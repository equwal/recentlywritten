bm-html: remove the live preview

Most sites do not let a page of another site show them in a frame: they
send X-Frame-Options or a frame-ancestors policy. YouTube is one of them.
For these sites the frame of the preview stayed empty, so the preview did
not work for most bookmarks.

bm-html is again the page of 0.5: a filter box, and the complete list
without script. The fzf preview of bm in a terminal stays: it shows the
text of the page, which bm-page gets with curl. The test "bm-html shows
no page in a frame" replaces the test of the preview pane.
