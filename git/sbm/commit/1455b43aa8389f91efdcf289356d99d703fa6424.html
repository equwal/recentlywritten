Windows: no right-to-left reordering in the fzf screen

The preview of bm can show Arabic or Hebrew text of a page. mintty then reorders the whole screen line, and the text leaves the preview box. The shortcut now starts mintty with Bidi=1, which turns off the reordering on the alternate screen, where fzf runs. The normal screen keeps it.
