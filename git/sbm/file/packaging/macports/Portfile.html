12 description         Bookmark manager made of POSIX sh scripts, driven by fzf
13 
14 long_description    sbm keeps bookmarks in one tab separated file: URL, \
15                     description, tags. bm picks one with fzf and opens it, \
16                     copies it, edits it or deletes it. Text that is no \
17                     bookmark is opened as an address or searched for on the \
18                     web. The rest are small filters over bookmark lines: \
19                     bm-import, bm-check, bm-html, bm-migrate, bm-title and \
20                     bm-commit. Everything is POSIX sh and POSIX utilities; \
21                     nothing is compiled.
22 
23 homepage            https://github.com/equwal/sbm
