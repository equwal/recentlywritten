1 sbm keeps bookmarks in one tab separated file: URL, description, tags. Lines
2 starting with # are ignored, so cut, grep, awk and an editor work on it as
3 well as the tools do.
4 
5 bm picks a bookmark with dmenu, or with fzf on a bare terminal, and then
6 opens it, copies it, edits it or deletes it. Text that is no bookmark is
7 opened as an address or searched for on the web.
8 
9 The rest are small filters that read and write bookmark lines: bm-import
10 turns a browser's bookmarks into bookmark lines, bm-check reports dead links,
11 bm-html writes a searchable web page, bm-migrate converts the old file
12 format, bm-title prints a page's title and bm-commit keeps the bookmark
13 file's history in git.
14 
15 Everything is POSIX sh and POSIX utilities. Nothing is compiled.
