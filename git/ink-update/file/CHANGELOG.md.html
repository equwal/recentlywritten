7 - Watches Rebind, Ink Recents, Ink Dim and Ink Update. It shows only the ones
8   that are installed.
9 - Looks in F-Droid, then Google Play, then GitHub.
10 - Makes one notification for each app that has an update. Tap it for the page,
11   or tap "Download" for the APK.
12 - A daily check that lives through a restart. You can turn it off.
13 - The intent action `dev.equwal.inkupdate.CHECK`.
