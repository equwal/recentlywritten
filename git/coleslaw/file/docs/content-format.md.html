2 # The Content Format
3 
4 Coleslaw expects content to have a file extension matching the class
5 of the content. (I.e. `.post` for blog posts, `.page` for static pages, etc.)
6 
7 There should also be a metadata header on all files
8 starting and ending with the config-specified `:separator`, ";;;;;" by
9 default. Example:
10 
11 ```
12 ;;;;;
13 title: foo
14 tags: bar, baz
15 date: yyyy-mm-dd hh:mm:ss
16 format: html (for raw html) or md (for markdown)
17 excerpt: Can also be extracted from content (see :excerpt-sep config param)
18 ;;;;;
19 your post
20 ```
21 
22 Posts require the `title:` and `format:` fields.
23 Pages require the `title:` and `url:` fields.
24 
25 To omit a field, simply do not have the line present, empty lines and
26 fields (e.g. "tags:" followed by whitespace) will be ignored.
