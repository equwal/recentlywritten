About

Spenser Truex. Free tarballs.

Tools for language learners, e-ink readers and Android, small Unix programs, and Common Lisp. Support the work at ko-fi.com/truex.

Sites

  • subread.space - Line up an audiobook with its ebook. Get read-along subtitles, EPUB 3 read-along books, and subtitled video. Free in the browser.
  • honjimaku.com - A library of Japanese subtitles for audiobooks, and Subrep live captions by share link.
  • sbmsync.com - sbm Sync: one plain bookmark file, the same on every device. Free, AGPL.
  • hentaibun.online - Reading lists and tools to learn kanbun and kobun.
  • recentlywritten.com - Writing on Unix, Lisp, Nix, Esperanto, and language learning. (equwal.com goes here.)
  • ko-fi.com/truex - Support.

SubRead

Read along with audiobooks, and mine the words.

  • subread.space - Read an .srt one line at a time with the space bar. Works with Yomitan, furigana, and pitch accent markup.
  • subplz-web - The aligner behind subread.space. The subtitle text comes from your book, not from a transcript.
  • subread-android - SubRead on Android. Times an audiobook against its ebook on the device.
  • subread-extension - Show your own .srt over a YouTube video, in time. Chrome and Firefox.
  • subread-overlay - Show .srt lines over any Android media player. Tap a word to look it up.
  • subread-dictionary - Pop-up dictionary for Android. Reads Yomitan dictionaries, with local audio.
  • subread.koplugin - KOReader plugin. The book follows the narration, from an .srt made by subread.space.
  • subrep-android - Live captions of the phone’s sound, made on the phone by Whisper, shared by link.
  • desktop-subtitle-replay - Live subtitles for anything on your screen. Replay a sentence and mine it into Anki.
  • honjimaku - The honjimaku.com server. Fork of jimaku.
  • kikiyomi - Audiobook player for Japanese learners, in the browser. Fork of kikiyomi.
  • Hoshi-Reader-Android - Fork with a subread branch: audiobook read-along in the Hoshi Reader EPUB reader.
  • chimahon - Fork with a readalong branch for the Mihon immersion reader.
  • dickt.store - Bring-your-own-key Yomitan dictionary converter.
  • vibeslop-dickt - Make your own Yomitan dictionary in a few minutes.

Rebind suite

Hardware buttons for e-ink readers and Android 12+.

  • rebind - Remap volume keys, Power, page-turn buttons, and the Viwoods AI key. Tap, double tap, hold, and combinations. Free beta APK.
  • assistkey - Key remapper for the Viwoods AiPaper Reader: AI key, volume keys, and Power.
  • awesome-rebind - Extensions and companion apps for Rebind.
  • ink-recents - Recent-apps switcher for e-ink. No animation. Swipe up to close.
  • ink-dim - Set the Viwoods frontlight below the lowest system level.
  • ink-update - Tells you when Rebind, Ink Recents, or Ink Dim has a new version.

sbm

Fuzzy search thousands of bookmarks, on every device.

  • sbm - dmenu bookmarks. Search and plumb them.
  • sbm-android - sbm on Android. Share to add.
  • sbm-extension - sbm for Firefox and Chrome.
  • sbm-sync - Sync server. One small Go program, plain files, AGPL.
  • sbm-suckless - sbm before the rewrite. Suckless style.
