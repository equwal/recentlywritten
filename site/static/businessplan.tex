\section{Overview}

\paragraph{We sell devices ThinkPad laptops with modified
firmware. Interested parties include programmers, Free Software advocates,
users and organisations concerned about backdoors or spying in device
firmware. A very large order from a three letter agency or military
organisation could be very valuable. The NSA has backdoors in the ``Intel
ME'', which is deleted entirely on Libreboot machines.}

\paragraph{Librebooted laptops have improved security capabilities, thanks to
installing a Free bootloader in the BIOS chip, and can boot encrypted
/boot partitions. They are guaranteed to function fully with 100\%
free drivers in the \textbf{FSF certified Parabola and Trisquel linux
distributions}, making them the best Linux laptops available.}

\paragraph{Librebooting devices is a much more involved skill than many users are
willing to develop, since the most powerful supported devices require
complete disassembly, a BIOS update, some shell commands, CPU
repasting, and installation of Linux. However, \textit{use} is not any
more difficult than using an Ubuntu machine.}

\section{Pricing}
\paragraph{To test the market, some T500 thinkpads were purchased from eBay sellers
at about \$100 each. Parts were upgraded, raising the price to
approximately \$200 per laptop. They were sold for \$405 plus shipping.}

\paragraph{The two competitors have their own internet shops for users to purchase
similar devices at \$750, and are often out of stock. We plan to compete
with their shops by offering private purchasing with Monero, a higher
volume of lower cost devices sourced from computer recyclers, and better
price discrimintation.}

\paragraph{We will produce multiple models at two tiers, to maximize profit through
price discrimination. Competitors waste valuable materials  by only
selling top-tier products.}

\subsection{T500 TOP-TIER PRODUCT \$750}
\paragraph{The CPUs will be
    upgraded with a quad-core modification and soldered wire, or a 3.06Ghz
    dual-core will be used not offered by competitors. 8gb RAM, 256 Gb SSD and
    9-cell battery. \$750 (match competitor's price, but ours is better). Good
    device plastics. 1650x1050 or better screen resolution.}

\subsection{T500 LOW-TIER PRODUCT \$500}
\paragraph{2.54 to 2.93 Ghz CPUs, 4Gb RAM, 6-cell battery, 120Gb
SSD. 1280x800 or better screen resolution.}

\paragraph{The replaced CPUs and RAM from top-tier boards can be repurposed for
low-tier sales.}

\section{Shop features}
\paragraph{Our shop will offer debit payments via Stripe and Paypal, but also offer
Monero and BTC. We expect these crypto payments to see significant use
considering the market, and competitors don't offer them.}

\paragraph{We will also offer the ability to order computers to be built for a
significant markup on each changed option. Competitors do this, but they
are wasteful by requiring buyers to ship their own computers to the UK
to be worked on. Instead, we will have buyers select from our currently
unimproved inventory.}

\section{Complementary and Supplementary Goods}
\paragraph{Replicant OS "phones", which also have free firmware, are often
desirable. They don't have cellular capabilities, are slow, and are very
inconvenient to use. However, some users will want these.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \small Cost: \$70, one hour of time labour.
    \item \small Sale: \$150 \normalsize
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Because Librebooted machines add additional cost, many users will opt
to purchase proprietary ThinkPads and other devices with reasonably good
Linux compatibility. This is why a competitor offers to Libreboot machines
mailed in for \$150 and 2-way shipping to the UK. We can compete by
offering the same service in the US (with lower shipping for Americans).}

\section{Getting The Word Out}
\paragraph{Currently, many individuals know about Libreboot and Replicant because
they are the only truly "Free Software" machines, and the only machines
without three letter agency backdoors. Probably the biggest popularizer
is Luke Smith (https://lukesmith.xyz) who has YouTube videos about them.}

\paragraph{Because the current pool of customers is highly knowledgeable, it seems
that a referral kickback program for people to put on their websites
might be a good method.}

\paragraph{A lot of free advertising is probably also available through Free Software
oriented groups like the FSF who might feature us in their materials.}

\section{Obstacles to Success}
\subsection{Labour costs}
\paragraph{The most powerful laptops (T500 or W500) cost 2-5 hours to produce. With
some special methods, it may be possible to reliably produce them at 2 hours.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \small Firmware update time: 30 minutes \normalsize
    \item \small Disassembly: 30 minutes \normalsize
    \item \small Re-pasting, part upgrades, soldering a quad-core mod wire: 30 minutes \normalsize
    \item \small Reassembly: 30 minutes \normalsize
\end{itemize}
