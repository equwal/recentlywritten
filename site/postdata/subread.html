SubRead makes it possible to read books with synced subtitles that keep your place. This is very nice for language learning.

Subread is a suite of tools that port forced alignment to a variety of platforms. It is 100% free when used locally, although it is possible to do conversions with my cloud for a fee. It is 100% self-hostable free software. BUY NOW!

Projects

  • subread.space: Read an .srt one line at a time with the space bar. Works with Yomitan, furigana, and pitch accent markup.
  • subplz-web: subread.space source
  • subread-android: subread.space android app (local and cloud conversions are possible)
  • subread-extension: Extension to use subread on youtube for audiobooks published there.
  • subread-overlay: Android app to show the subtitles as an overlay on the phone. Can be used with the dictionary for a very nice AJATT experience.
  • subread-dictionary: Yomitan pop-up dictionary standalone android app. Works really good with the overlay, but can be used anywhere!
  • subread.koplugin: KOReader plugin that does live highlighting of the book while you listen to it in another app like Voice, VLC, or MPV.
  • subrep-android: Live whisper captions. Better than the ones that come with android.
  • desktop-subtitle-replay: Whisper on your computer, and broadcast them to your friends. Useful for online language classrooms or bilingual exchanges.
  • honjimaku: Book subtitles database for japanese
  • Hoshi-Reader-Android: Fork of hoshi reader that can do the conversion inside hoshi reader. Hopefully gets merged.
  • chimahon: Fork with a readalong branch for the Mihon immersion reader.
  • dickt.store: Bring-your-own-key Yomitan dictionary converter.
  • vibeslop-dickt: Make your own Yomitan dictionary in a few minutes.

Why

Extremely useful for learning languages by reading books with the audiobook.
